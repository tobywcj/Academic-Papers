## University of Edinburgh: MSc Data Science

### Composed Image Retrieval in Fashion Using CLIP-based Multimodal Fusion
*MSc dissertation, School of Informatics, 2024 · 54 pages*

Composed Image Retrieval (CIR) retrieves a target image from a query made of a reference image and a text modification. The dissertation runs an ablation study of state-of-the-art CIR methods to isolate which components actually drive performance. It compares linear and non-linear multimodal fusion of CLIP image and text features, and tests a two-stage training scheme. On the FashionIQ and Shoes benchmarks, two-stage training on top of the fusion strategies gives a significant gain in retrieval accuracy.

`CLIP` · `multimodal fusion` · `composed image retrieval` · `FashionIQ` · `Shoes`

### Realistic Sketch to Image Synthesis using RePainting-based Diffusion Model
*Machine Learning Practical group project (G016), final report, 2024 · 9 pages*

A conditional diffusion model, built from scratch, that turns sketches into realistic images using RePainting-based sampling, as an alternative to GAN-based methods such as SketchyGAN and pix2pix. The team also built a paired sketch–photo dataset covering three classes (clothes, cars, dogs). Results are evaluated at 64×64 with LPIPS, FID and Inception Score.

`diffusion models` · `RePaint` · `image synthesis` · `sketch-to-image`

### Personalized Recommender Systems under Artificial Intelligence Breakthrough
*Informatics Research Review, January 2024 · 13 pages*

A critical review of how AI has changed recommender systems, focused on data sparsity and scalability. It covers deep learning frameworks from published research and from industry systems such as Google and Netflix. It also looks at the shift from traditional collaborative filtering to models that capture richer user behaviour, and the role of open-source work. Future directions include ethical AI and multimodal personalisation.

`recommender systems` · `deep learning` · `personalisation` · `literature review`

## University of Leeds: BEng Electronic & Electrical Engineering

### Modelling and Control of Bidirectional DC-DC Converters for Electric Vehicles
*Final year project report, 2022 · 72 pages*

Design and simulation, in MATLAB/Simulink, of a non-isolated half-bridge bidirectional DC-DC converter linking an EV battery to the high-voltage DC bus. The project covers:

- **Soft switching:** Zero Voltage Resonant Transition (ZVRT) to improve power transfer efficiency.
- **Voltage regulation:** PWM and PI control schemes, implemented and compared.
- **Drive control:** feedback speed control of a DC motor through the converter, analysed under motoring, acceleration and regenerative braking.
- **Ripple reduction:** an interleaved multiphase topology to cut high-frequency switching ripple.

`power electronics` · `electric vehicles` · `bidirectional DC-DC converter` · `PI control` · `Simulink`
